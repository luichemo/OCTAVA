## Brand & Style

This design system channels the tactile energy of DIY screen-printed gig posters, risograph art zines, and raw rehearsal-space notices. Built for musicians seeking collaborators worldwide, it merges physical ink textures with modern mobile swipe mechanics. The experience feels punchy, expressive, and unvarnished—abandoning slick, generic matchmaking tropes in favor of tactile paper layers, high-contrast flat inks, deliberate misregistration overlaps, and punchy musical badges.

The visual tone sits at the intersection of Neo-Brutalism and Riso-Print Printmaking:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation:** Off-white newsprint backdrops layered with hyper-saturated spot inks (Hot Pink, Canary Yellow, Cyan Aqua) outlined in rich Deep Navy ink.
- **Graphic Misregistration & Texture:** Mechanical offset drop shadows, intentional half-tone dot fills on cards and badges, and raw typographic stamps (e.g., `JAM!`, `AUDITION`, `PASS`).
- **Global & Cross-Script Respect:** Designed with deep structural generosity to comfortably support both Latin scripts and script systems with distinct vertical metrics and expanded text lengths, specifically Georgian (Mkhedruli).

## Colors

The palette simulates vibrant multi-pass spot ink printing on tactile paper stock. Rather than relying on traditional gray scales, deep ink navy provides structural clarity and outline weight, preventing visual mud while amplifying primary action hits.

### Light Palette (Print Stock Mode)
- **Ink Navy (`#22328C`):** Primary structural stroke, high-contrast borders, solid typography, hard offset shadow casts.
- **Paper Canvas (`#EEF0EC`):** Textured off-white foundation representing raw unbleached print stock.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FAFBF8`):** Elevated foreground surface for profile swipe stacks, cards, and bottom sheets.
- **Hot Pink Spot (`#FF4FA3`):** Primary catalyst. High-priority call-to-actions, mutual-interest badges, primary interaction hits.
- **Canary Yellow (`#FFE14D`):** Secondary accent. Match notifications, highlight tags, stamp overlays, audio wave indicators.
- **Cyan Aqua (`#74D3EA`):** Tertiary accent. Instrument chips, genre filters, and secondary toggle states.
- **Paper Blush Tint (`#FFC9E2`):** Low-contrast ink wash for chip backgrounds, secondary state containers, and inactive audio tracks.

### Dark Palette (Backstage Gig Mode)
- **Void Navy Canvas (`#10173A`):** Dark foundation replacing the paper stock.
- **Amplify Navy Surface (`#18214D`):** Elevated cards, input fields, swipe card backs.
- **Strobe White Text (`#E8EBF8`):** Crisp textual foreground maintaining strict contrast with background ink.
- **Ink Linework:** Transitions from deep ink to `#E8EBF8` or pure `#FF4FA3` on active components, retaining hard solid drops.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs raw display impact with systematic reading legibility:

- **Headline Hierarchy:** Rendered in condensed, all-caps display typography evocative of bold wood-block concert posters. Letter-spacing is slightly loosened to preserve punchiness even at reduced scales. 
- **Body & Structural Text:** Relies on robust neutral sans typography with open apertures and neutral proportions. Line-heights are intentionally set to generous metrics (`1.55` - `1.65`) to seamlessly accommodate Georgian Mkhedruli script characters, which feature distinctive ascenders, descenders, and vertical loops that quickly collide in tighter editorial settings.
- **Language Adaptability:** English headlines default to high-impact condensed uppercase. For Georgian strings where vertical condensed casing is not culturally native, headlines dynamically drop to bold geometric grotesque weights while keeping the identical structural line-height and bounding bounds.
- **Stamps & Badges:** Use dedicated tracking and uppercase styling to emulate rubber stamps and physical press print marks.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a dynamic fluid grid system grounded in tactile rhythm:
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 639px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 16px gutters. The primary swipe canvas occupies full viewport height minus the 80px pinned action deck. Touch boundaries prioritize thumb-reach zones.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) margins and gutters. Single-column card stacks center within a maximum constrained reading frame of 480px, flanked by quick-filter drawer panels.
- **Desktop / Wide Viewports (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Features split-screen dual architecture: active discovery deck pinned to a central 520px container with live audio stems, chat threads, and filter chips occupying collateral columns.

Element spacing strictly follows an 8-point base cadence (with a 4px fine-adjustment sub-tier). Touch targets maintain an absolute minimum height and width of 48px to allow aggressive, confident interactions in handheld environments.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on diffused, realistic shadows or Gaussian blurs, this design system articulates elevation exclusively through **Hard Screenprint Offset Shifting** and **Heavy Ink Linework**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** Flat `#EEF0EC` (Light) or `#10173A` (Dark) with optional 5% opacity dot halftone screen patterns applied across the background canvas.
- **Level 1 (Static Surface Containers):** 2px solid `#22328C` border with an unblurred offset drop: `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #22328C`.
- **Level 2 (Cards, Interactive Modules, Badges):** 2px solid `#22328C` border with a 4px hard offset drop: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #22328C`.
- **Level 3 (Swipe Cards & Dragging States):** During active card swipes, elevation intensifies: `box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #22328C`, with the card rotating dynamically between `-6deg` and `+6deg`.
- **Color-Cycled Offset Casting:** High-emphasis action buttons break monochromatic shadows by casting offset drops in contrasting ink colors—for example, a Hot Pink button casting a Canary Yellow hard shadow (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #FFE14D`).
- **Interactive Depress State:** On tap or click, buttons physically shift into their shadow by `translate(2px, 2px)` while decreasing the drop shadow to `1px 1px 0px`, simulating mechanical punch-card feedback.

## Shapes

The design system employs a roundedness token scale of `2` (Moderate Rounded), blending crisp 16px (`1rem`) to 24px (`1.5rem`) corner radii with structural rigidity:

- **Swipe Cards & Main Surfaces:** 24px corner radius (`rounded-xl`), framed with an uninterrupted 2px ink border.
- **Action Buttons & Floating Stems:** Full pill radii (9999px) paired with rigid 2px borders, producing an energetic, hand-held feel.
- **Instrument, Role, & Genre Chips:** Pill-shaped capsules with generous horizontal padding, preventing tight typographic clipping when rendering complex multilingual character strings.
- **Stamps & Badges:** Angular or lightly curved (4px – 8px), rotated between `-3deg` and `+4deg` to suggest stamped ink stickers manually applied to equipment cases.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Swipe Buttons (Pass / Jam):** Circular 64px x 64px minimum triggers. 2px solid `#22328C` border. Reject/Pass defaults to `#FAFBF8` background with `#22328C` icon and a 4px `#22328C` shadow. Jam/Match defaults to `#FF4FA3` background with white icon and a 4px `#FFE14D` contrasting hard shadow.
- **Standard Button Pills:** Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px (`px-6`). Bold uppercase display typography. Micro-interaction: `active:translate-x-0.5 active:translate-y-0.5 active:shadow-none`.

### Cards & Profiles
- **Profile Swipe Stack:** Rounded-xl (24px), background `#FAFBF8`, 2px solid `#22328C`, 4px hard shadow. Top visual slot features full-bleed photography treated with subtle duotone or high-contrast grain. Overlaid with an audio preview waveform bar.
- **Audio Stem Mini-Player:** Embedded within cards. Hot Pink progress track over an unbleached `#EEF0EC` groove, delineated by 2px ink borders.

### Tags, Chips & Filters
- **Genre & Skill Badges:** Pill-shaped, height 36px, `px-4 py-1.5`. 2px border `#22328C`. Unselected: `#FAFBF8` background. Selected: `#74D3EA` (Cyan) or `#FFE14D` (Yellow) fill with a 2px offset shadow.
- **Vibe / Looking-For Chips:** Soft pink fill (`#FFC9E2`) with `#22328C` ink text and 1.5px border.

### Inputs & Search
- **Text & Audio Upload Fields:** Surface `#FAFBF8`, 2px solid `#22328C` border, 16px corner radius. Placeholder text in muted ink navy with 60% opacity. Focused state shifts the shadow to `4px 4px 0px #FF4FA3`.
- **Multilingual Support:** Padding accommodates Georgian line ascents without baseline cropping (top/bottom padding minimum 14px).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 24px x 24px rounded square (6px radius), 2px ink border. Checked state fills with `#FF4FA3` featuring a bold custom graphic white cross/check.
- **Radio Buttons:** 24px circular container, 2px solid ink. Selected state displays an inner concentric `#22328C` dot with a 4px `#FFE14D` ring offset.

### Match Overlay & Reaction Stamps
- **"IT'S A JAM!" Modal:** Fullscreen halftone paper backdrop with dual profile portraits overlaid at 5-degree counter-angles.
- **Feedback Watermarks:** As cards are dragged left or right, prominent diagonal rubber stamps (`PASS` in `#22328C`, `JAM!` in `#FF4FA3`) fade in and scale up at 15-degree angles.
