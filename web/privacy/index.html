Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

OCTAVA is an app that helps musicians find each other and form bands. It is run by an individual developer based in Georgia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: .

Who can use OCTAVA

You must be at least 16. People aged 16–17 can use OCTAVA, but they are never shown to adults in the feed and cannot have one-on-one chats with adults; they can only meet adults inside bands of three or more people.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
  • Date of birth: used only to check that you are 16 or older and to apply the age rules above. Other people see your age, never your date of birth.
  • Profile: name, area, instruments and skill levels, genres, influences, goals, how often you rehearse, availability, gear, what you are looking for, links you add, and your band roles.
  • Audio and images: the audio clips you record or upload, and your avatar (an illustration we generate on request; we never make photos of people).
  • Location (optional): if you choose to share it, your position rounded to about 1 km. Others only see an approximate distance.
  • Activity: who you swipe on, your matches, one-on-one and band chat messages (text and voice), message edits, read marks, band memberships and votes, rehearsals, songs and setlists.
  • Safety: blocks and reports you make or that are made about you, including any reported message text, and moderation decisions.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device token so we can send push notifications, and the app language for their text.

We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs and do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your profile to suitable musicians, find matches, deliver messages and run bands.
  • To keep people safe: enforce the age rules, act on reports, hide or suspend accounts that break our terms.
  • To send notifications you have allowed (messages, matches, band activity, rehearsal reminders).
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

Our legal bases are providing the service you asked for (contract), keeping the service safe (legitimate interests), and your consent where we ask for it (location, notifications, avatar generation).

Who can see what

  • Your profile, clips and avatar are visible to other signed-in musicians in your age group, and to your bandmates.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in that chat. Moderators can see a reported message. When you report someone you have a one-on-one chat with, a copy of their last 20 messages to you is kept with the report so a moderator can review it.
  • If your band turns on its public page, anyone with the link can see the band's name, profile, clip, ready songs and members — adults with name and avatar, members aged 16–17 only as their instrument.

Service providers

We use these companies to run OCTAVA; they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database, login, file storage, real-time chat), hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Cloudflare (Workers AI) to generate avatars from your main instrument and genres. No photos or personal details are sent.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and Apple Push Notification service on iPhones) to deliver notifications.
  • GitHub Pages to host the website and the web preview.
  • Telegram: when someone files a report, a summary (names, reason and any reported message) is sent to the moderator's private Telegram chat.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Delete account), we delete your profile, clips, avatar, matches, messages, swipes, blocks and reports, and remove you from your bands, straight away. Bands you leave keep the content other members added. Earlier versions of edited messages are kept only while the message exists, to handle reports.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app at any time, stop sharing your location, turn notifications off in your phone settings, and delete your account in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it, by writing to . We will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to Georgia's Personal Data Protection Service or your local data protection authority.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make sure people can only read what they are allowed to, and files are private and shared through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
